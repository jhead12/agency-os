Panels

A plugin panel (plugins/panels/) is a card on the dashboard or on every prospect's page. The app draws the card and its title; your template is only what goes inside, so use the classes above (detail-row, badges, a small table). On the prospect page people can move, resize, color and hide it like the built-in cards. See "Panels" in docs/PLUGINS.md.
